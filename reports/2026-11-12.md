- **Google Cloud:** 14 free-text click-and-collect lines ("2 dozen eggs", "1.5 lb chicken thighs", "a baker's dozen
  bagels", "750ml olive oil, two bottles") → one query, `AI.GENERATE(..., endpoint => 'gemini-4-hydrogen',
  output_schema => 'item STRING, quantity FLOAT64, unit STRING')` next to the default `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`. Every
  hydrogen answer must come back typed with a unit in {each, g, ml}; agreement with a hand-worked key (within 1 %) is
  reported.
- **Databricks:** three store price files (15 rows, 59.35) in a managed volume with a comment and a `READ VOLUME`
  grant. The serverless job snapshots it (SHA-256 per file, rows / price total read with Spark, comment, grant), drops
  it, finds it in `SHOW VOLUMES DROPPED` **by the ID recorded at creation** (an earlier same-name drop is listed too),
  runs `UNDROP VOLUME WITH ID`, and snapshots again; both snapshots must equal the local reference and the volume must
  keep its ID and comment. Locally: reference, ID/identifier validation, row picking, a local pyspark read of the files
  and the REST client against a fake API.
