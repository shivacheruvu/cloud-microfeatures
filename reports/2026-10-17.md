| Databricks | Basket and aisle allergens | `collect_union` aggregate (Databricks SQL 2026.35) | built |

- **Databricks:** 18 products with Open Food Facts-style allergen arrays in 5 aisles, 12 baskets in two stores.
  One read-only query rolls the arrays up per basket and per aisle with `collect_union`, next to the long-hand
  `array_distinct(flatten(collect_list(...)))`, and flags nut baskets with `arrays_overlap`. Local: Python set
  unions, cross-checked in the test with DuckDB `list_distinct(flatten(list(...)))`. **2 of 12** baskets
  allergen-free, **5** with nuts, gluten in **8**, **3 of 5** aisles nut-free. The cloud check fails on any group
  whose allergen set differs or where `collect_union` disagrees with the long-hand version.
