- **Databricks:** 4 stores and 60 households on a 10 km local metre grid (SRID 0). One read-only
  `INNER JOIN stores s ON st_dwithin(h.geom, s.geom, 2500.0)` over `st_makepoint` points returns every home-store
  pair within 2.5 km with its `st_distance`. Local: DuckDB cross join + Euclidean distance filter. **39 of 60**
  homes covered (49 pairs), **10** with a choice of stores, **21** outside every radius. The cloud check fails on any
  pair in one result only or a distance more than 1 m off; every home is at least 5 m from any radius boundary.

- **Google Cloud:** 12 weeks of daily scan counts for bananas, milk and sandwich bread at one store (8 weeks
  history, 4 weeks = 84 store-days checked) with three planted problems: a banana scanner outage (8 units vs ~237),
  duplicate milk scans (297 vs ~116) and a short bread delivery (36 vs ~81). One `AI.DETECT_ANOMALIES(TABLE
  scan_history, TABLE scan_target, ..., id_cols => ['product'], model => 'TimesFM 3.0')` checks all three series.
  Local weekday-median baseline (±30%): 3 of 3 caught, 0 false alarms. The cloud check fails if a point is missing
  or has a status message, or fewer than 2 planted problems are flagged; warns on 1 miss or more than 4 false alarms.
