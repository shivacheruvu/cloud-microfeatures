- **Google Cloud:** a promo property graph (3 brands, 8 products, 4 flyers, 14 flyer slots) with `MEASURE(SUM(...))`
  properties on products and flyers. Per brand, the naive `SUM` over flattened `GRAPH_EXPAND` rows says **4,680 weekly
  units; `AGG` over the measure says 2,470** (+89.5% overcount; Green Valley 2.19x because its oat milk sits on three
  flyers). Flyer reach is overcounted 30-76% the same way. A guard query checks `SUM` over a measure is refused.
- **Databricks:** the restock job's `trigger.job_completion` watches `receive-dc-truck` and `receive-dsd-vendors` with
  `if_status: SUCCESS` and reads `{{job.trigger.job_completion.completed_jobs}}`. A stdlib model of the documented
  rules replays a receiving day: **7 upstream runs → 2 restock runs** (the ASN re-run replaces the stale DC run, the
  failed vendor run is skipped, the pre-trigger run never counts; `DONE` → 2 runs, `FAILED` → 0); 120 cases received,
  88 straight to the shelf. The cloud run creates the three serverless jobs, runs both receiving jobs and checks the
  trigger hands over exactly those runs in configuration order; all jobs deleted on exit.
