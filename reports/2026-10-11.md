## Shipped

- **Databricks:** a shelf-price table (2 stores x 6 products) with row tracking on and no `enableChangeDataFeed`.
  INSERT, an UPDATE (milk +20 cents), a DELETE (north drops oat milk) and a MERGE of the weekly feed (1 price
  change, 1 new item, 2 unchanged rows), then one `table_changes(..., 0)` query. Locally, DuckDB runs the same
  DML and the change rows come from diffing snapshots: 20 change rows; since the tags were printed, **3 reprints,
  1 new tag, 1 removed**. The cloud check fails if the CDF property is set, or if any step's net change per item or
  the final table differs from local.
- **Google Cloud:** on 2026-09-01 kombucha moved to an endcap, oat milk to the bottom shelf, sparkling water stayed
  put. 84 days before and 28 after, daily, with a weekend pattern. One `AI.CAUSAL_EFFECT` call
  (`id_cols => ['product']`). Locally, a same-weekday-mean counterfactual and a z-test: kombucha **lift +42.5%**,
  oat milk **drop -29.5%**, sparkling water **no effect** (-0.2%, p 0.82). The cloud check requires the same class
  per product and an empty `status`; the size of the effect is a warning.
