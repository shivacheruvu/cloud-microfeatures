- **Google Cloud:** 10 online orders placed around the end of DST (2026-11-01) at stores in Chicago, Denver, Phoenix
  and New York. Exact-hour promises vs wall-clock maths: **7 of 10 promises are an hour off** on the wall clock, 5 fall
  due in the repeated 01:00 hour; Nov 1 had 25 h (Phoenix 24 h). `RegExp.escape` matches catalog names literally
  (`M&M's (Peanut)` 2 vs 1 unescaped), `Uint8Array.fromBase64` decodes the data with UTF-8 names intact. Must equal a
  Python `zoneinfo` reference and report Node.js 26.
- **Databricks:** `PATTERN ( strt down+ (flat | down)* up+ )` finds **4 temporary price reductions in 3 of 6 SKUs**
  over 21 days (deepest 22.3%, including a two-step cereal cut); `PATTERN ( strt same_trip* )` splits 84 loyalty scans
  on 8 cards into **22 shopping trips** at 45-minute gaps. `MATCH_RECOGNIZE` isn't in open-source Spark, so the local
  reference applies the DEFINE conditions per row and runs the PATTERN as a Python regular expression (leftmost,
  greedy, non-overlapping = `AFTER MATCH SKIP PAST LAST ROW`).

## What ran in the cloud
- After #128 merged: **Google Cloud day 35 failed, then fixed forward here.** The job ran Node.js 26 on the
  `nodejs26` image (`--command=node` worked, default job identity); `RegExp.escape` and `Uint8Array.fromBase64`
  worked, but **Node.js 26 has no `Temporal` global and no `Math.sumPrecise`** by default, so the delivery-time and
  total figures were missing. Fix: the job runs as `node --harmony-temporal` (plain `node` if rejected), uses Temporal
  when present and `Intl.DateTimeFormat` otherwise (cross-checking the two when both run), sums exactly in integer
  cents without `Math.sumPrecise`, and reports which engine ran. Both paths reproduce the reference on local Node 22.
  The cloud run after this PR merges decides `ran`.
