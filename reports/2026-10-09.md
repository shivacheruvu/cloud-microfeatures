- **Databricks:** 13 fiscal weeks x 2 stores, FY2026 W49 to FY2027 W09, on a dense `week_index`. The metric view
  defines `units_prev_week` (`range: current`, `offset: -1`), `units_prior_4wk` (`trailing 4`) and `units_to_date`
  (`cumulative`). Last week of FY2027 W01 is FY2026 W52 with no special case. Spikes (>1.25x prior 4-week average)
  are exactly the two seeded promo weeks; south's +42% week-over-week rebound after the holiday dip is 1.14x, not a
  spike. The local run rejects a non-dense index, since the platform would silently return wrong numbers.
- **Google Cloud:** one `ML.SEASONALITY` call (`seasonalities => ['WEEKLY']`, `id_cols => ['store']`) over 12 weeks
  of daily bread sales. Local classical decomposition: north peaks Saturday (+57 loaves over trend, trough Tuesday
  -33), south peaks Friday (+37). The cloud check requires the same peak weekday per store and an empty `status`;
  trough, correlation and amplitude differences are warnings. `bq` needs `--max_rows=1000` here (168 output rows).
