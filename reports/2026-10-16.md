- **Databricks:** 4 stores, each with an IPv4 `/24` and an IPv6 `/64`, and 40 lane devices (registers,
  self-checkouts, scales, handhelds) with their registered store and last reported address. `try_ip_host` cleans
  each address (3 long-hand IPv6 addresses become canonical, 2 junk values become NULL), and a `LEFT JOIN ... ON
  ip_cidr_contains(cidr, ip)` (guarded by `ip_version`) finds the store network each device is really on.
  Local: Python's `ipaddress` module. **34 of 40** ok, **2** in the wrong store, **2** on unknown networks,
  **2** bad addresses. The cloud check fails on any device whose canonical IP, version, store or status differs.
