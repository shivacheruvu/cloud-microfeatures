- **Google Cloud:** one Python definition produces both the graph DDL (3 aisles, 7 products, 12 restock scans; 7
  properties / measures with `OPTIONS(description=...)`, 3 `MEASURE`s) and a 12-column contract for `GRAPH_EXPAND`.
  In BigQuery, `CALL BQ.SHOW_GRAPH_EXPAND_SCHEMA` must report every contract column with exactly the 3 measures
  flagged (descriptions compared where returned), then a `GRAPH_EXPAND` + `AGG` rollup per aisle must equal the local
  model: 31 facings by `AGG`, 64 by a naive `SUM` over scans, 227 units.
- **Databricks:** a serverless job (environment version 6) parses 8 Open Food Facts-style payloads padded with nulls,
  runs `variant_strip_nulls(payload)` and `variant_strip_nulls(payload, false)`, and reports bytes and completeness:
  28 nulls removed, 1,722 → 1,382 JSON bytes (-19.7%), nutriment completeness 70%; every stripped payload equals a
  pure-Python strip. Local pyspark 4.3 runs the same SQL in the test and matches.
