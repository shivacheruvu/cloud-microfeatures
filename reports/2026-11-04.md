## What ran in the cloud
- After #114 merged: Databricks day 31 warned `FEATURE_DISABLED` like days 29-30 (verified locally only, nothing left
  behind). Day 23 (Files API) still ran; days 20-22 and 24 still fail on the warehouse / jobs.
- After #115 merged: **Google Cloud day 31 failed.** Bucket, uploads and the bad-cleanup deletes went through, then
  the first hard delete pause PATCH got **HTTP 412**; the annotation cut the reason off (multi-line JSON). Teardown's
  bucket delete then got HTTP 409 (not empty), so one `cmf-d31-` bucket with a few ~170-byte objects is left; the
  next run's leftover sweep retries it. Days 24-30 all ran as before.
- Fix forward (#116): errors print the JSON API reason on one line, the pause PATCH retries with a pause-only body
  (no retention field) if the first form is refused, and the cleanup warning says how many live / soft-deleted
  objects are left.
- After #116 merged: the reason came through. The pause **had** been set and the incident ran through the restores;
  the 412 was on **lifting** it: "Hard Delete Pause can only be disabled after the minimum duration has passed.
  Allowed after: <about 24 h after it took effect>", and the bucket delete's 409 was "the bucket you tried to delete
  has hard delete pause enabled". #116's teardown also restored and re-deleted soft-deleted objects in rounds, which
  under the pause only piled up tiny generations (64 and 413 in the two kept buckets, a few KB).
- Fix forward (#117): the minimum-duration refusal is now an expected, checked outcome (its "allowed after" time is
  recorded as hours after the pause took effect); teardown empties and keeps a paused bucket, and later runs delete
  earlier `cmf-d31-` buckets once their minimum has passed. Two buckets from today stay until about 2026-11-06 04:00
  to 06:00 UTC.
- After #117 merged: **Google Cloud day 31 ran.** The pause read back enabled with an effective time; the bad cleanup's
  5 files were listed soft-deleted, a soft-deleted file read gave 404, the 4 good files came back with their original
  MD5 (24 price rows), the draft stayed deleted, live files 6 = plan; lifting the pause was refused until 24.0 h after
  it took effect, as now expected, so that run's bucket is kept (emptied) and the earlier two were kept too. The
  first later run after about 2026-11-06 04:00 UTC deletes all three.
