  the 7 price-lookup misses and 14 anonymous shoppers stay NULL, revenue $366.44. Finding: on pyspark 4.3 the
  non-Arrow path fails on the same DataFrame (`CANNOT_MERGE_TYPE`); serverless always uses Arrow. The local test runs
  the real call on local pyspark and matches the pure-Python reference.
- **Google Cloud:** 7 store price files, a bad cleanup soft-deletes 5, hard deletion is paused, the 4 good files are
  restored from their soft-deleted generations (24 price rows, MD5 checked), the unapproved draft stays deleted, the
  pause is lifted. The local test runs the whole runner against a fake JSON API, including teardown and an
