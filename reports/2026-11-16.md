- **Google Cloud:** `supplier_terms.py` writes Harbor Farms' two-page trading terms as a PDF byte for byte (8
  sections: ordering, delivery, prices, returns / recalls, cold chain, payment, shortage claims), each holding the
  exact phrase that answers a clerk's question ("within 48 hours of delivery", "41 F or below" ...). One query chunks
  it with `AI.CHUNK_DOCUMENT` at `chunk_size` 100 and 500; the run must find every term at both sizes on pages 1-2
  with no errors, and reports how many terms sit whole in one chunk on the right page.
- **Databricks:** a serverless job creates `supplier_case_costs` with a table comment and four column comments, then
  reloads it for week 47 with `CREATE OR REPLACE TABLE ... AS SELECT` (adds `unit_cost` and `week`, names no
  comments) and compares `information_schema` before and after: comments must be kept and rows must equal the
  reference (3 new case costs, total $160.85, cheapest unit HF-3001 at $2.39). Locally open-source Spark 4.3 gives
  the same rows through a drop + CTAS but loses every comment.
