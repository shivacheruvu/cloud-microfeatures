- **Google Cloud:** five synthetic store scan exports (30,927 bytes of CSV, UTF-8 names) compressed in one query at
  levels -5 / 1 / 3 / default / 19 and read back with both decompress functions: **5.64x at level 3, 6.25x at 19**;
  a header-only export grows 47 → 56 bytes. Every BigQuery frame must decompress locally with plain libzstd.
- **Databricks:** 12 task parameters such as `{{(job.start_time + 3.days).iso_date}}` and
  `{{(job.start_time - 1.month).month}}` feed a markdown plan: 13 lots due by the cutoff → **2 pull, 5 at 50% off,
  3 at 25% off (298 units), 3 keep**. A stdlib resolver for the documented syntax passes the 4 documented examples
  and 6 edge cases (month-end clamping is an assumption: not documented). The cloud run creates the job, runs it once
  on serverless and compares all 12 values with the resolver for that run's own start time; job and scratch deleted.
