- **Databricks:** 22 grocery items, 5 out of stock. One read-only `INNER JOIN shelf t EXACT NEAREST 2 BY DISTANCE
  <mismatch score>` (no `ON` clause) returns two in-stock substitutes per out-of-stock item. The score: 1000 for a
  different category, 1 per cent/100 g of price difference, 1 per 10 g of pack difference, 50 for organic vs. not.
  Local: DuckDB cross join + `row_number()` + `QUALIFY`. All **5** first choices are same-category (median score 5);
  lime sparkling water's second choice is almond milk (1172) because there's only one other sparkling water.
  The cloud check fails on any substitute, rank or score that differs from local; the data has no ties at rank 2/3.

- **Google Cloud:** 84 days of daily strawberry sales (weekday pattern, drift, ±6% noise, 11 promo days at ~1.6x)
  and 14 held-out days with 3 promo days. `AI.FORECAST(model => 'TimesFM 3.0', target_cols => ['units'],
  future_covariate_cols => ['on_promo'], horizon => 14)` gets the history plus 14 horizon rows with NULL units and
  the known promo flag (held-out sales never sent). A second query runs it univariate. Local: seasonal naive MAE
  **19.9** units/day (**72.9** on promo days), a promo-aware baseline (weekday mean x 1.58 lift) **6.9** (**10.8**).
  The cloud check: every day forecast and positive, and TimesFM-with-promos beats seasonal naive; warnings if the
  covariate doesn't beat univariate TimesFM or TimesFM is over 1.5x the promo-aware baseline.
