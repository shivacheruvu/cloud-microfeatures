  unqualified basket report gives $95.12 on the regular path and $86.20 with the ad-week schema first (`shelf_price`

  skipped, a one-part name (`INVALID_SQL_PATH_SCHEMA_REFERENCE`) and a duplicate entry (`DUPLICATE_SQL_PATH_ENTRY`) are
