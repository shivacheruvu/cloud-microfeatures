  saving truncates toward zero (honey: -0.29, where `floor` gives -0.30). Four baskets void their last N scans with
  `trim_array(scans, voids)`, one down to an empty receipt. Local: pure-Python exact fractions. The cloud check
  fails on any label, rounded value, saving, kept list or total that differs. Read-only, nothing created.
- **Google Cloud:** a product master with `product_key INT64 GENERATED ALWAYS AS IDENTITY (START WITH 1000
  INCREMENT BY 10)`. Load 8 products, add the vendor feed's 2 new items (2 known ones skipped), try a hand-written
  key (must fail), read `INFORMATION_SCHEMA.COLUMNS`, and key 10 scan events. Locally DuckDB uses a sequence.
  BigQuery keys are only unique and sparse, so the check is on properties: same products, no repeats, all on the
  1000 + 10n grid, hand-written key refused, same units / scans per product with the right key.
